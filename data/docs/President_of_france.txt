Emmanuel Macron is the current President of France, serving in office since May 14, 2017.
Overview of the OfficeRole: Executive head of state and commander-in-chief of the armed forces.
Residence: Élysée Palace in Paris.Term 
Length: Five years, limited to two consecutive terms.
Background and Early CareerBirth: Born December 21, 1977, in Amiens, France.
Education: Studied philosophy and graduated from the École Nationale d'Administration (ENA) in 2004.
Early Work: Worked as a financial inspector and an investment banker at Rothschild & Cie Banque.
Political CareerGovernment Posts: Served as deputy chief of staff to François Hollande and later as Minister of Economy, Industry, and Digital Affairs from 2014 to 2016.
Rise to Presidency: Founded the political movement En Marche! (now Renaissance) and won the 2017 presidential election at age 39.
Re-election: Won a second term in the 2022 presidential election.
